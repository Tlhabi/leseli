# Leseli – Monitoring & Evaluation toolkit (for Deliverable C)

Most of the numbers below are **collected automatically by the app** (Teacher area → *Tekolo le tlhahlobo*) and exported as CSV.
Only the classroom observations and interviews are collected on paper.

## 1. Problem → change we expect (theory of change)

**IF** learners at Moshoeshoe II can practise the topics they find hard (long division, multiplication, angles, oxygen,
animal classification, English letters), in their own language, on a phone that needs no data, with step-by-step help
and their teacher's voice, **AND** teachers can teach from one phone and answer questions,
**THEN** learners will spend more time on these topics and score better on them, and learners with disabilities and girls will take part equally,
**BECAUSE** the barriers the school reported (no devices, no data or budget, shared textbooks, no help at home) are removed.

## 5. Data collection plan

| What | How | When | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline | In-app **starting check** (10 items per grade) before first use | Pilot day 1 | Learners, supervised by a teacher |
| Usage | Automatic event log (no names) | Continuous | App |
| Learner usability | Smiley faces after lessons + *Re bolelle* form (voice notes allowed) | Continuous / end | Learners |
| Teacher usability | In-app **SUS** (Sesotho/English) | End of week 1 and week 3 | Teachers |
| Endline | In-app **final check**, same items | After 2–3 weeks | Learners |
| Observation | Paper sheet: engagement, who uses the phone, problems | 2 class-mode lessons | Student team |
| Interviews | 5 short interviews (principal, 2 teachers, 2 learners with guardian consent) | End | Student team |

Export: Teacher area → M&E → *Share summary* / *Learner results CSV* / *Usage log CSV* / *Feedback & SUS CSV*. The CSVs open in Excel or Google Sheets.

## 6. Pilot design (8–15 users)

* 10 learners (Grade 5–7; mix of girls and boys; include learners the school identifies as needing support, with guardian consent) and 3 teachers.
* Week 1: training and baseline. Weeks 1–3: use (class mode at least 2× per week, home use where possible). Week 3: endline, SUS, interviews.
* **Iteration cycles**: after week 1, collect feedback (*Re bolelle*, mistake reports, inbox, observations), change the app, and log it in `CHANGELOG.md` with photos (with consent) and screenshots. Repeat after week 2.

## 7. Honest limitations to discuss in the report

* Small sample, no control group, and a short pilot: a gain cannot be attributed to Leseli alone (teachers also teach!).
* Same items in baseline and endline → some gain may come from familiarity with the test.
* Usage data only counts phones running Leseli; learners sharing a guest profile are not tracked individually.
* Self-reported smileys from children may be positive out of politeness.
* Risk of unintended consequences: more screen time; learners without phones may be left out (mitigate with class mode and sharing).
