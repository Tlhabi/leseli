# Leseli – Tataiso ea mosebelisi · User guide

*Mantsoe a makhutšoanyane, litšoantšo tse kholo. Short words, big pictures. Sesotho first, English below.*

---

## 1. 📲 Ho bula Leseli · Opening Leseli

**ST:** Tobetsa letšoao la Leseli (mokorotlo o mosehla) fonong. Ha ho hlokahale inthanete kapa data.
**EN:** Tap the Leseli icon (the yellow mokorotlo) on the phone. No internet or data is needed.

The first time only: **choose a language → choose help (seeing / hearing / big text) → read the privacy page → "Kea utloisisa".**
Lekhetlo la pele feela: **khetha puo → khetha thuso → bala leqephe la lekunutu → "Kea utloisisa".**

## 2. 👧🏾 Ke mang ea ithutang? · Who is learning?

| | Sesotho | English |
|---|---|---|
| ➕ | Tobetsa **Kenya moithuti**. Ngola lebitso la bosoasoi (eseng lebitso le felletseng), khetha setšoantšo le sehlopha (5, 6, 7). | Tap **Add a learner**. Type a nickname (not your full name), pick a picture and your grade. |
| ✅ | Mosuoe kapa motsoali o netefatsa hore motsoali o saenne foromo ea tumello. | The teacher or parent confirms that a guardian signed the consent form. |
| 🔁 | Fono e le 'ngoe – baithuti ba bangata. Tobetsa lebitso la hau nako le nako. | One phone, many learners. Tap your own name each time. |
| 👤 | **Moeti**: ho leka feela – tsoelo-pele ha e bolokoe. | **Guest**: just to try. Progress is not saved. |

## 3. 🏠 Leqephe la lapeng · The home page

- **Sehlopha 5 / 6 / 7** at the top switches the grade. · Holimo: fetola sehlopha.
- **Lithuto** (subjects): Lipalo, Saense le Theknoloji, Senyesemane, Sesotho, Botho le Sechaba, Boqapi le Khoebo.
- 🤔 **Lelotho la kajeno** and 💬 **Maele a kajeno**: a new riddle and a new proverb every day.

## 4. 📘 Thuto · A lesson

1. Tobetsa thuto → **Lintlha** (notes). · Tap a lesson → **Notes**.
2. 🔊 **Mpalle / Read to me**: the phone reads the part aloud and lights up the words.
3. 🎙️ **Lentsoe la mosuoe / Teacher's voice**: if your teacher recorded this part, you hear their voice.
4. 🛠️ **Leka / Try it**: tools you can touch, such as a protractor, fraction bars or an experiment.
5. ⭐ **Araba lipotso / Take the quiz**: up to 3 stars. A wrong answer shows the right answer and *why*.
6. ⚑ A red flag means *baithuti ba re sena se thata* (learners said this is hard). Go slowly.

## 5. 🦉 Botsa Thuso · Ask Thuso

| Ngola · Type | Thuso e etsa · Thuso does |
|---|---|
| `7452 ÷ 6` · `345 x 23` · `4587 + 9765` | Shows **every step**, with the bus stop / column drawing |
| `1/2 + 1/3` · `2 1/2 - 1 3/4` | Works out fractions step by step |
| `round 45678` · `triangle 50 60` · `250 cm to m` · `20% of 150` | Rounding, angles, units, percentages |
| `water in Sesotho` · `pula ke eng ka Senyesemane` | Translates words |
| `what is oxygen?` · `oksijene ke eng?` | Finds the answer in the lessons |
| `mpotse` · `quiz me` | Asks you a question |

🎤 **Maekrofono**: speak your question. If the phone cannot understand speech, Thuso **records your question for the teacher**. The teacher answers, and you see the answer in **Nna → Lipotso tsa ka**.

## 6. 📚 Laeboraring · Library

**Buka ea mantsoe** (word book) · **Lipale** (stories, with "Mpalle buka eohle" to have the whole book read to you) · **Libuka tsa thuto** (textbooks) · **Lipapali** (Morabaraba, lilotho, spelling bee, quick maths) · **Maele le lilotho**.

## 7. ⚙️ Litlhophiso · Settings (Nna → Litlhophiso)

| | |
|---|---|
| 👁️ **Thuso ea ho bona / Seeing support** | Big text, every page read aloud. Works with TalkBack. |
| 👂 **Thuso ea ho utloa / Hearing support** | Green flash = right, red flash = wrong, the phone vibrates. Nothing depends on sound. |
| 🔤 Mongolo o moholo · Text size, high contrast, easy-read spacing, less movement | |
| 🗣️ Lebelo la ho bala · Reading speed | |

## 8. 📝 Liteko · Checks

**Teko ea ho qala** (starting check) at the beginning, and **Teko ea ho qetela** (final check) after some weeks. They have the same 10 questions, so we can see whether Leseli helps. *Hase teko ea sekolo* – it is not a school test.

## 9. 🔒 Lekunutu · Privacy

Tsohle li lula fonong. Ha ho letho le romeloang inthaneteng. Leseli ha e na tumello ea inthanete ho hang.
Everything stays on the phone. Nothing is sent to the internet, and Leseli does not even have internet permission.
