# Leseli – Teacher guide and 1-hour training · Tataiso ea mosuoe le koetliso ea hora e le 'ngoe

For the 6 teachers of Moshoeshoe II Primary School. The questionnaire said the best time is the school holidays.
*Bakeng sa basuoe ba 6 ba Moshoeshoe II. Nako e molemo ke phomolong ea sekolo.*

## Training plan (60 minutes, one phone per 1–2 teachers)

| Time | Activity · Ketso | Teacher does · Mosuoe o etsa |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Why Leseli? We show what the school told us (no lab, data costs, shared textbooks, hard topics). *Hobaneng Leseli?* | Listens and adds what we missed |
| 5–10 | Install it (APK from a phone by Bluetooth, or the web link). Open it; choose language and access settings. | Installs on their own phone |
| 10–20 | **Learner side**: add a test learner → Lipalo → *Ho arola (bus stop)* → Mpalle → stepper tool → quiz | Completes one lesson and quiz |
| 20–25 | **Thuso**: type `7452 ÷ 6`, `water in Sesotho`, record a voice question | Asks 3 questions |
| 25–30 | **Teacher area**: create a PIN. Never share it with learners. | Sets a PIN |
| 30–38 | **Class mode**: pick a lesson, show slides, ask "hands up for A/B/C/D", reveal the answer, then enter the number of learners | Teaches a 3-slide mini lesson to the group |
| 38–45 | **Record your voice**: Teacher area → *Rekota lithuto ka lentsoe la hau* → record one Sesotho section | Records 1 section |
| 45–50 | **Inbox**: listen to the test voice question and reply by text or voice | Replies |
| 50–55 | **Correct the lessons**: fix one Sesotho word, then *Romela litokiso* | Makes 1 correction |
| 55–60 | **M&E**: show the KPIs, the starting check, and *Share summary* by WhatsApp. Fill in the SUS survey. | Completes the SUS (10 questions) |

## Daily use ideas · Mehopolo ea letsatsi le letsatsi

- **Morning, 10 minutes**: Class mode with the *Lelotho la kajeno* (riddle of the day) and 5 quick questions.
- **Maths block**: show the bus-stop stepper in class mode, then learners practise in pairs on the teacher's phone during group work.
- **Homework**: learners who have a phone at home (the questionnaire said "some") use Leseli in the evening. Learners without one can use a parent's phone, because profiles keep each child's progress separate.
- **Learners with disabilities**: turn on Seeing or Hearing support on their profile's phone. Record Sesotho narration for the lessons they need first.
- **Answer the inbox** once a week. Learners see your reply in *Nna → Lipotso tsa ka*.

## Looking after Leseli (sustainability) · Ho hlokomela Leseli

- **Who**: the Principal is the contact person (questionnaire Q23). One teacher is "Leseli champion" each term.
- **Updates**: a new version arrives as a new APK file by Bluetooth or WhatsApp. Learner progress stays on the phone.
- **Backup**: Teacher area → *Backup le lintlha* → *Etsa faele ea backup* at the end of each month. Keep the file private.
- **Sharing content**: lessons you write can be shared with other schools (*Arolelana lithuto tsa ka*).
- **Cost**: M0 per month. No data, no server, no licence fees. Phones and electricity are the only costs, and the school already has both.

## Safeguarding · Tšireletso

- Learners use **nicknames**. Never type full names, phone numbers or photos into Leseli.
- A **guardian consent form** (`CONSENT-FORMS.md`) must be signed before a learner is added.
- If a voice question reveals abuse or danger, follow the school's child-protection procedure and the **Child Helpline 116** / **Police 112**.
