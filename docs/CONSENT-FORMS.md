# Consent forms · Liforomo tsa tumello

Print two copies: one for the family, one for the school. Do not add a learner to Leseli before the form is signed.

---

## A. Tumello ea motsoali / mohlokomeli · Parent / guardian consent

**Projeke:** Leseli – thuto ntle le inthanete (Limkokwing University, DIIC3110 ICT4D) le Sekolo sa Mathomo sa Moshoeshoe II.

**Sesotho**
Re kopa tumello ea hore ngoana oa hau a sebelise app ea Leseli fonong ea sekolo kapa ea lelapa nakong ea teko (libeke tse ka bang 3).
- Ngoana o tla sebelisa **lebitso la bosoasoi** feela. Ha ho nkuoe litšoantšo tsa hae ntle le tumello e ngoe e ikhethileng.
- App e boloka feela tsoelo-pele ea hae (lithuto, lintlha tsa lipotso) **fonong**. Ha e romele letho inthaneteng.
- Re tla sebelisa linomoro tse kopaneng (ntle le mabitso) tlalehong ea rona ea sekolo.
- Ngoana a ka khaotsa nako efe kapa efe, ntle le bothata. Lintlha tsa hae li tla hlakoloa ha u kopa.
- Ha re tšepise thepa kapa chelete. Ka mor'a projeke app e tla sala ho sekolo mahala.

**English**
We ask permission for your child to use the Leseli app on a school or family phone during a trial (about 3 weeks).
- Your child will use a **nickname** only. No photos will be taken without separate written consent.
- The app keeps only learning progress (lessons, quiz scores) **on the phone**. It sends nothing to the internet.
- We will use combined numbers (no names) in our course report.
- Your child may stop at any time without any problem. Their data will be deleted on request.
- We do not promise equipment or money. After the project the app stays with the school, free of charge.

## B. Tumello ea moithuti · Learner assent (read aloud to the child)

*Re etsa app e thusang bana ho ithuta. U ka e leka haeba u batla. Ha u sa batle, ho lokile – ha ho kotlo. U ka emisa neng kapa neng.*
*We are making an app to help children learn. You can try it if you want to. If you don't want to, that is fine – there is no punishment. You can stop any time.*

## Interview guide (10 minutes)

1. How did you use Leseli this week? (*U sebelisitse Leseli joang bekeng ena?*)
2. What helped the most? What was difficult?
3. Did the Sesotho make sense? Which words should change?
4. Did any learner who usually struggles take part more, or less?
5. What would stop the school using Leseli after we leave?
6. If you could change one thing, what would it be?
