# Change log and iteration record

The brief requires evidence of at least **two iteration cycles based on user feedback**. Iterations 0–2 below were done by the
team before the pilot. **Iterations 3 and 4 must be filled in with REAL feedback from Moshoeshoe II users** (keep this honest; the marker will check).

## Iteration 0 – needs assessment → design (week 10–11)
Source: principal's School Needs Questionnaire (30/10/2026), learner questionnaire (15 learners), photos of the syllabus.
- Chose an offline Android app (no lab, phone data only, no budget) over SMS/USSD (cannot carry lessons/diagrams) and over a local content hub (needs power in classrooms and hardware the school doesn't have).
- Language: Sesotho + English together (principal: "both").
- Focus content on the hard topics learners named. Added accessibility because "disabled learners" need the most support and the school asked us to be careful with "vulnerable students and their levels of understanding".
- Classmates' requests added: AI help in every module (Thuso), recorded voice questions, notes, books, dictionaries, textbooks.

## Iteration 1 – v1.0 prototype (week 12)
- 80 lessons in Grades 5–7, quizzes, Thuso, practice, library, games, teacher area, M&E.

## Iteration 2 – v1.1, team testing on a 360×740 phone screen (week 13)
| Problem found | Change |
|---|---|
| Class-mode slides stayed on top after leaving the screen | The overlay now closes on every page change, and the screen-awake lock is released |
| English line under the lesson title was unreadable on the coloured header | Higher contrast for secondary text on coloured backgrounds |
| "Prefer not to say" button squeezed in the add-learner form | Buttons wrap onto a new line |
| Protractor numbers ran the opposite way to the green arm | Scale reversed so the reading matches the arm |
| `*italic*` marks appeared as stars and were read aloud | Italic formatting is now rendered, and the stars are stripped from speech |
| Focus outline boxed every page title | Removed for programmatic focus (kept for keyboard users) |
| Service worker would fail inside the Android app | Only registered in the browser/PWA version |
| Long-division drawing did not show the "bring down" number | Layout shows each brought-down number and the remainder |
